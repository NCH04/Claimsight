The metric was watching the wrong thing

60 undamaged vehicles. Share the damage classifier correctly called no damage — measured on the whole photo, then on the part crops cut from those very same photos.

Trained on whole photos only Trained on both domains

The two models are indistinguishable on whole photos — 98% vs 100%, and overall accuracy moved only 0.787 → 0.806. On the crops the pipeline actually classifies, the gap is 16% vs 96%: an 84% false-positive rate that no validation set was measuring. Training on both domains closed it to 4%.
